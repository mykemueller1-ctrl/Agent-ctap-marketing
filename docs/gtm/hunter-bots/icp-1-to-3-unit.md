**Status:** living lab + public sources. Not a 5–50 unit CEO persona.  
**Specimen (VERIFIED):** Community Tap & Pizza, Fort Dodge, IA — one store, owner-operator, PDQ, photo invoices, Hy-Vee liquor Monday, ops mail at `communitypizza2026@gmail.com`.  
**Product match:** Free 3P audit → free Action Shift (1 store / 1 login) → Owner ~$199 → Command only if they grow past this ICP.

Hunter hunts **this person**, not “restaurants,” not Taco Bamba’s 16-unit CEO, not R365 buyers.

---

## Who they are (operator, not buyer persona fluff)

They run the floor and the books. The back office is them on a laptop at 11 p.m. (Never86 Owner-role language — matches CTAP.)

Typical shape:

- **1 store**, sometimes a second, rarely a clean third
- Full-service pizza, bar, diner, fast-casual, neighborhood QSR — the concept varies, the **job** does not
- They are GM, HR, marketing, and AP
- They have a computer in the office where **nightly POS closes already land**
- Vendors show up with paper or photos more than EDI (Sysco 1×, PFG 2×, local meat/beer 2–8×)
- They already pay for a POS and 3P. They are **burnt on SaaS** that wanted a demo, a contract, and a login they never open

**Not this ICP:** 5–50 unit CEOs/CFOs/COOs (those are Command / design-partner). 20-location franchisees. Dashers. Consumers. Vendors selling software.

## Economics they live (guardrails, not claims)

CTAP alcohol study (internal, sales-grounded): beer cost target **<21%**, liquor **<20%**, food **<30%**, labor **<28%**. A $1,400 weekly alcohol buy can be fine against $13k alcohol sales; full par-fill is the cash trap.

3P: commission is not all-in. Ads, refunds, error charges, promos sit under it. Never call overcharge without the contract.

These are **coaching rails**, not nationwide averages. Don’t quote them as “the industry.”
