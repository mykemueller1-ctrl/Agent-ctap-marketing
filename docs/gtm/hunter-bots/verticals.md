# Vertical knowledge — where 1–3 unit operators actually complain

Research baseline: Never86 Aug 14–20 public 3P signal sample (Facebook operator-origin first), `r/restaurantowners` viral DoorDash owner rants, LinkedIn restaurant-finance mechanism posts, TikTok carried cases. Hunter copies that map. It does not fake accounts.

## 1. Independent pizza (CTAP analog)

**Why we win:** PDQ nightly Z-report already lands in an inbox. Food + Large Pizza is one food number. 3P is the free wedge.

**Listen:** pizza owner FB groups, Reddit pizza + restaurantowners, X “I own a pizza shop.”

**File:** redacted DoorDash statement, then a Z-report photo.

**Language they use:** “DoorDash is a second restaurant,” “ads ate it,” “payout doesn’t match the tablet.”

## 2. Neighborhood bar

**Why we win:** invoice photos (Humes, Northern Lights, Sawyer) + Human Insight next action, not another invoice warehouse.

**Listen:** bar owner groups, beer distributor complaint threads.

**File:** 3P if they deliver; else invoice photo. Never ask for distributor portal logins.

**Language:** “keg invoice doesn’t match what we poured,” “Hy-Vee liquor run every Monday.”

## 3. 1–3 unit QSR / fast casual

**Why we win:** free Action Shift seat. They’re burnt on SaaS.

**Listen:** r/restaurantowners, TikTok owner-facing delivery rants.

**File:** one marketplace statement.

**Drop:** 20-location franchisee war stories unless they clearly own 1–3 themselves.

## 4. SaaS refugees (MarginEdge, R365, 7shifts, HotSchedules, Sling)

**Why we win:** they already have an invoice DB. They don’t have tomorrow’s one move.

**Listen:** LinkedIn + X “cancelled MarginEdge,” “R365 too expensive for one store.”

**Hook is not “we replace them.”** Hook is next-action coaching on top of files they already get.

## 5. Iowa / Midwest trust lane

**Why we win:** Victor / On the Line 515. Not a bot.

**Listen:** Iowa owner chatter, Feedwell-adjacent, Fort Dodge / DSM independents.

**Move:** human intro. Hunter only flags. Outreach does not impersonate Victor.

## 6. Accountants / bookkeepers (not ICP, amplifiers)

They want a month-end method, not a viral post. Use the earned-authority accountant note, not the Hunter hook. One guide link. Ask for a review, not a testimonial.

## Channel rank (effort vs signal)

1. Facebook independent-owner groups (highest real operator voice; human browses, Gronk scores pasted URLs)
2. Reddit r/restaurantowners (public, Grok can search)
3. X (Grok native — Head of Marketing home)
4. TikTok (carried cases; treat as demand signal, not audited loss)
5. LinkedIn (mechanism / accountant / SaaS refugee)
6. Google (`"I own a restaurant" DoorDash`) for stragglers

- **MarginEdge:** invoice OCR DB. We are not “better OCR.” We are next action.
- **R365:** accounting suite. Later Command-depth, not day-1.
- **Toast / PDQ / Square:** POS. We parse the close they already email.
