## How these deals actually work (so we don't waste shots)
- **National beer** (Yuengling, Coors, Bud, Modelo) → **through your distributor rep**, NOT the brand HQ. One email to your rep covers all of them. Ask: co-op $, tap handles, swag, a branded night.
- **Iowa/regional craft** → **email the brewery directly** (events/taproom contact). They love tap-takeovers and will bring kegs + swag + sometimes a rep.
- **Liquor** → Iowa is a control state (no wholesale deal), but **brand ambassadors** support features with POS, branded cups, and sampling nights. Iowa distilleries partner locally like craft breweries do.

> **Send plan:** personalized DRAFTS staged for Myke to review + send (or approve a batch) —
> not an auto-blast of 20 strangers (protects Gmail deliverability + the CTAP name). Verify
> each public email before sending; national HQs often only have a partnership web form.
